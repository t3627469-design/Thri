

1.3.x additions (placement guard, instinct speed, build up)
- Placement guard: blocks are placed so the result matches the schematic state where the click can decide it (log axis,
  stair/slab/trapdoor half, button/lever face). A right block in the wrong state (e.g. stair facing the wrong way) is
  detected, broken and re-placed, at most twice per block; block types that can't be fixed from where you stand are left
  alone and a warning is shown.
- speed 6 = instinct: no delay, instant aim, one block per tick. only-build defaults to 6.
- Build up: blocks too high to reach are now reached for both only-build and human-builder.
  Creative: it flies to a spot in reach (and repositions by flying).
  Survival: it builds a pillar of spare blocks (cobblestone, dirt, ...) beside the build, climbs it, steps onto the layers
  already built and keeps building from up there. The pillar stays until the build is done, then it is mined down again.
